Unmasking True Retail Valuation: How Our Proprietary Buy Timing Score™ & Product Analysis Work

Have you ever purchased an online product only to witness its price plummet by 40% the very next week? During seasonal shopping events like Black Friday and Prime Day, mega-retailers notorious for artificial price markup scams inflate list prices just before slapping on fake "50% OFF" badges. Here is how our custom-engineered Product Analysis suite and algorithmic evaluation model protects your wallet from artificial surges.

The Buy Timing Score™ Algorithm (0–100): Our proprietary mathematical formula evaluates current live listings against trailing 30, 60, and 90-day historic minimums. Scores above 80 earn a green "Good Deal" verification, while inflated prices instantly trigger a red "Wait" recommendation.
Multi-Platform Asynchronous Audit: When viewing an item, the client fires concurrent REST queries to our backend, parsing Google Shopping, Amazon, eBay, and specialty vendors simultaneously to expose the absolute lowest verified live URL in under 300 milliseconds.
One-Tap Smart Price Drop Monitors: Integrated directly into the product analysis screen is our automated Smart Alert radar. With interactive quick-target chips (-5%, -10%, -20%), users can deploy dedicated 24/7 cloud cron workers to monitor rates automatically.
